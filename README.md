### 🗂 Data Dictionary (ApexPlanet Sales Dataset)

| 🏷️ Column Name | ⚙️ Data Type | 📖 Meaning | 💼 Business Relevance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Order_ID** | `Text / String` | A unique identifier for each order. | Used to count total orders and track specific transactions. |
| **Order_Date** | `Date / Time` | The date when the customer placed the order. | Used to analyze monthly or yearly sales trends and seasonality. |
| **Customer_ID** | `Text / String` | A unique identifier assigned to each customer. | Used to identify new or returning customers and track loyalty. |
| **Customer_Name** | `Text / String` | The full name of the customer. | Used for personalized marketing and customer relationship management. |
| **Age** | `Numeric (Float)` | The age of the customer in years. | Used to understand customer demographics and age-based purchasing behavior. |
| **Gender** | `Text / String` | The gender of the customer (Male or Female). | Used to analyze product demand and preferences based on gender. |
| **City** | `Text / String` | The city where the customer is located. | Used to identify top-performing regions and geographic sales distribution. |
| **Product** | `Text / String` | The specific item purchased by the customer. | Used to identify top-selling products and manage product-level inventory. |
| **Category** | `Text / String` | The broader category of the product (e.g., Electronics, Grocery). | Used to analyze profitability and overall category performance. |
| **Quantity** | `Numeric (Int)` | The number of items purchased in a single order. | Used for inventory management and understanding demand volume. |
| **Unit_Price** | `Numeric (Float)` | The selling price of a single unit of the product. | Used for analyzing pricing strategies and calculating revenue. |
| **Total_Sales** | `Numeric (Float)` | The total bill amount (Quantity × Unit_Price). | Used to calculate the company's total revenue and financial growth. |
